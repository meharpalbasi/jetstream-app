Privacy

Effective 30 September 2026

Jetstream has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. It doesn't collect your name, email, location or contacts, and it asks for no permissions.

Who we are

Jetstream is made by Meharpal Basi ("we"). For anything about this policy, email meharpalbasi45@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your settings: the tower you last tuned, the fader levels, the choices in Settings, and whether you've seen the timer hint. They're stored on your device and never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app sends, and to whom

adsb.lol, for the traffic

While the app is on screen or the tower is playing, Jetstream asks adsb.lol's public API, about every 10 seconds, for the aircraft within 40 nautical miles of the airport you've tuned. The request contains the airport's coordinates, never yours. Like any website, adsb.lol receives your device's IP address with each request; we receive nothing.

aviationweather.gov, for the weather

At most every 10 minutes per airport, Jetstream asks the US National Weather Service's aviationweather.gov for the airport's current weather report (METAR), so the tower can read out the wind and altimeter setting. The request contains the airport's code and, as above, your IP address reaches the server.

Apple and RevenueCat, for Jetstream Premium

Apple handles every purchase; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account. To check whether Premium is active and to restore it on your devices, Jetstream uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a random ID made on your device (not tied to your name or Apple Account), your App Store purchase records, the App Store country, your preferred languages, the app, iOS and device model versions, and an identifier Apple gives each app maker for your device (the "identifier for vendor"). RevenueCat keeps the purchase records against the random ID so Premium works, and gives us totals such as how many people subscribed. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Nothing else

The tower's voices, the radio sound, the lo-fi and the cabin noise are all on your device or made on it. Jetstream doesn't use your location, contains no advertising or analytics code, and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites. Its Live Activity updates on your device, without push notifications. Siri, Shortcuts and the App Store are Apple's, under Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell or share personal information, and we hold none: the only records connected to you are the anonymous purchase records described above.

Children

Jetstream is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold about you and ask us to delete it. We hold nothing linked to you. To have your anonymous purchase record removed from RevenueCat, email us the order ID from your App Store receipt so we can find it. If you're in the UK or the EU, you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

This website

These pages are static files hosted by GitHub Pages. They set no cookies, run no analytics and load nothing from other sites (the fonts are served from here). GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses to keep the service secure; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If a change affects what the app sends, the app's App Store listing will change with it.
