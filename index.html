Jetstream

A tower frequency to work to. Tune an airport's tower and you hear a controller working the traffic around it, with lo-fi and airliner cabin noise underneath and a radar of the aircraft it's talking to.

About the towerThe tower you hear is generated from live flight data. It isn't real air traffic control, and Jetstream never plays a recording of a real frequency.

In the app

  • 18 towers, from New York JFK, London Heathrow and Gatwick to Tokyo Haneda, Sydney and Brasília, each with its own controller. Heathrow and Gatwick talk the UK way.
  • The scope. A radar of the traffic around the airport, with real runway layouts. The rim glows green while someone transmits; tap a blip to see what it is and what it's doing.
  • Captions. One quiet line under the scope shows who's talking and what they say.
  • The mix. Radio, Lo-fi and Cabin faders. Jetstream makes the lo-fi on your device as it plays, so it never repeats and works offline.
  • Flights. A focus timer framed as a flight: 25, 50 or 90 minutes, ending with a cabin chime and a fade.
  • Everywhere you work. Background playback, lock screen controls, a Live Activity, Siri and Shortcuts, and a two-column layout on iPad in landscape.

Free and Premium

Jetstream is free with three towers: New York JFK, London Heathrow, and a tower of the day that changes daily. Captions, the scope, the mixer, Siri and the Live Activity are free too.

Jetstream Premium adds all 18 towers (and new ones as they're added), flights, and the Quiet and Busy frequencies. It's a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, bought through the App Store; the app shows the price before you buy. How subscriptions work

Questions

Is this real air traffic control?

No. The aircraft on the scope are real, from live ADS-B data, but everything you hear is generated: Jetstream writes the calls a tower would make to those aircraft, and synthetic voices speak them through a modelled radio. Never use it for flying or anything operational.

Why does the scope say SIM?

When the flight data can't be reached (you're offline, or the feed is down), Jetstream switches to simulated traffic and says so: SIM in the status row, and "Traffic · simulated" under the scope. It switches back when the data returns.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The voices, the radio and the lo-fi are all on your device. Offline, the tower talks to simulated traffic and invents its weather.

How do I restore Premium on a new device?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account, then use Restore Purchases in the app.

How do I cancel the subscription?

In your Apple Account's subscriptions (on iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions). Premium stays on until the end of the period you've paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Jetstream collect my data?

No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. The privacy policy lists every request the app makes.
