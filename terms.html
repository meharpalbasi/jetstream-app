What Jetstream is for

The tower you hear is generated from live flight data. It isn't real air traffic control.

  • Jetstream is for listening while you work, study or relax. Every call on its frequency is generated by the app and spoken by synthetic voices; none of it is a real transmission or instruction.
  • Don't use Jetstream for flying, flight planning, navigation, air traffic services or anything else operational or safety-related.
  • The aircraft on the scope come from volunteer-fed ADS-B data (adsb.lol) and the weather from aviationweather.gov. That data can be late, incomplete or wrong. When it can't be reached, the app shows simulated traffic, marked SIM.
  • Airline names appear only as the radio callsigns that identify flights in that data. Jetstream isn't connected with, or endorsed by, any airline, airport or air navigation service.
